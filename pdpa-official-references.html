Reading or viewing personal information

Hanya dengan membaca atau mengakses maklumat sudah dianggap “pemprosesan”.

Source: JPDP - Soalan Lazim, under the question about when an organization processes personal data.

The corresponding official English explanation is available in the JPDP English FAQ. Applicability still depends on the facts and current law; supplying a viewer for authorized users is not itself a determination of controller or processor status.

Official notice-preparation guidance

JPDP notice-preparation guidance (Bahasa Malaysia) provides official guidance and a downloadable notice template. A template must be completed for the actual organization and processing; another organization's notice should not be copied as DMS Viewer's own policy.

JPDP notice-preparation guidance (English) and the official quick guide explain notice requirements, including the language requirement where applicable.

Legislation and current materials
